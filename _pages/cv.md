## Research Interest

My research interests focus on intelligent wireless communication system cross-layer optimization, deep learning-based channel access, network optimization, and PHY-layer algorithm design in Wi-Fi systems, as well as PHY-MAC layer resource allocation based on non-convex optimization theory in mobile edge computing.

* **5G O-RAN Engineer Intern**, Celona Inc., Cupertino, CA. Summer 2022.
