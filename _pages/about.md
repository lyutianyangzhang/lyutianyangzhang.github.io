Lyutianyang Zhang (章吕天阳) received his B.E. degree in electronic communication from Beijing Institute of Technology in 2017. He received the M.Sc. degree and Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering from the University of Washington, Seattle, WA, USA, in 2019 and 2023, respectively. He is currently an Assistant Professor in the School of Microelectronics and Communication Engineering at Chongqing University.

He is an official voter and active contributor in the IEEE 802.11bn (Wi-Fi 8) Working Group, focusing on PHY and MAC standardization. Previously, he worked as a Wi-Fi Performance Software Engineer at Apple in Cupertino, CA, where he focused on PHY/MAC performance evaluation and optimization for commercial wireless products. His research focuses on wireless network optimization for Wi-Fi, 5G, and 6G AI-RAN; Wi-Fi 8 standardization; and hardware platforms for 6G AI-RAN and Wi-Fi systems for large language model (LLM) applications.

## Research & Professional Service

* **Research:** Wireless network optimization for Wi-Fi, 5G, and 6G AI-RAN; Wi-Fi 8 standardization; and hardware platforms for 6G AI-RAN and Wi-Fi systems for LLM applications.

* **Standardization:** Official voter and active contributor in the IEEE 802.11bn (Wi-Fi 8) Working Group.

* **Journal Reviewer:** IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications (TWC), IEEE Transactions on Communications (TCOM), IEEE Communications Standards Magazine, IEEE Internet of Things Journal (IoTJ), among others.

* **TPC Member:** IEEE Global Communications Conference (GLOBECOM), IEEE International Conference on Communications (ICC), among others.

* **Invited Talks:** Invited speaker at the 2026 ICWOC & ISA Global LiFi Summit; invited keynote speaker at IEEE/CIC ICCC 2026, presenting *"Wi-Fi 9: An AI-Native Era."*

## 中文简介

章吕天阳于2017年获北京理工大学电子通信工程学士学位，并于2019年和2023年分别获美国华盛顿大学电气工程硕士与博士学位。现任重庆大学微电子与通信工程学院助理教授。

他是 IEEE 802.11bn（Wi-Fi 8）工作组正式投票成员和活跃贡献者，主要参与 PHY 与 MAC 层标准化。此前曾在美国库比蒂诺苹果公司担任 Wi-Fi 性能软件工程师，从事商用无线产品 PHY/MAC 层性能评估与优化。研究方向包括面向 Wi-Fi、5G 和 6G AI-RAN 的无线网络优化、Wi-Fi 8 标准化，以及面向 6G AI-RAN 与大语言模型（LLM）应用的 Wi-Fi 硬件平台建设。
